How a scan works

  1. Answer a few questions.How your skin feels, your main concern, your current routine, and whether you wear SPF and makeup.
  2. Take a bare-faced selfie in daylight.Face a window, no filter, phone at eye level. Same time each day gives the cleanest trend line.
  3. Get your glow score in seconds.Four metrics, two observations, one tip. Then a routine built around them.
  4. Come back tomorrow.Day to day scores wobble. The trend over weeks is the part that tells you something.
